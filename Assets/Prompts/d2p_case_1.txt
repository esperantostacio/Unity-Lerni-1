## 8. LIKELY DIAGNOSIS (FOR EVALUATOR, NOT PATIENT ROLE)

Most likely diagnosis:
- Acute tonsillitis/pharyngotonsillitis
- Consider infectious mononucleosis as differential

Supporting constellation:
- Sore throat with painful swallowing
- High fever (39.5 C)
- Neck swelling sensation (possible lymph nodes)
- Marked fatigue
- No classic cold symptoms (no cough/runny nose)

Differentials:
- Infectious mononucleosis (EBV)
- Peritonsillar abscess
- Viral pharyngitis
- Scarlet fever (streptococcal)

Suggested further steps:
- Physical exam (throat/tonsils/lymph nodes)
- CBC, CRP, possibly monospot
- Throat swab (rapid strep)
- Analgesic/antipyretic (ibuprofen/paracetamol)
- Antibiotics if indicated by findings
- Sick leave recommendation
- Hydration and rest
