## 5. PRELIMINARY DIAGNOSIS AND DIFFERENTIALS

### Suspected diagnosis:
- NSTEMI (non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction)
- Rationale: elevated troponin + ST depression V1/V2 + typical symptoms (retrosternal tightness, left-shoulder radiation, nausea) + cardiovascular risk factors (smoking, diabetes, positive family history)

### Differential diagnoses:
1. Unstable angina (no troponin rise would be expected)
2. Posterior STEMI (less likely with unremarkable V7-V9)
3. Aortic dissection (missing classic signs)
4. Pulmonary embolism (limited support)
5. Hypertensive emergency with angina (admission BP was hypotensive)
